FAQ

Quick answers

Questions we get every day.

How do I reserve a car?

Message us on WhatsApp or book a test drive. A sales advisor confirms availability and explains the reservation steps.

Do you offer bank finance or hire purchase?

Yes, both are available. Tell us your budget in the form above and we'll explain the options for the car you want.

How does importing work?

Share your requirements, get an estimated landed cost, pay a 20% deposit, and we search and bid in the source country. Once you pick a car you pay a further 40%. We then handle shipping, clearance and delivery.

How long does registration and transfer take?

Ask your advisor for the current timeline on the specific car. It depends on the vehicle and paperwork.

Where are your showrooms?

Southern Office Tower, Two Rivers on Limuru Road, and near Motorhub on Kiambu Road, Nairobi.
